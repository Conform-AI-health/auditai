## Intended purpose

AuditIA is an exploratory Clinical AI evaluation project focused on learning, testing, and demonstrating safety-oriented approaches to AI-assisted medical auditing.

## Explicitly outside scope

AuditIA is not presented as a system for autonomous medical decisions, autonomous coverage authorization or denial, replacement of qualified medical reviewers, direct patient-care decisions, or clinically/regulatorily validated deployment.

## Human oversight

Qualified human review remains central to the project. AI-generated support should not displace professional responsibility for high-stakes interpretation and decisions.

## Data boundaries

Public portfolio material is synthetic. Patient-identifiable information and confidential employer information must not be committed to this repository.

Any future real-world implementation would require appropriate privacy, security, access-control, auditability, retention, incident-management, and applicable LGPD safeguards.

## Risk domains considered

The project considers risk domains including unsupported inference, false certainty, inappropriate completion of missing information, logical inconsistency, context contamination, untrusted-source instructions, automation bias, inadequate escalation, and model/instruction drift.

## Design principle

**Traceability over fluency.**

For a high-stakes workflow, an auditable support output is more valuable than persuasive prose whose evidentiary basis cannot be reconstructed.

## Validation boundary

Synthetic testing can expose failure modes and guide development. It does not establish clinical validity, patient benefit, real-world safety, regulatory qualification, or fitness for autonomous decision-making.

## Responsible disclosure

If a security, privacy, or safety concern related to publicly released AuditIA material is identified, please contact the repository owner privately rather than publishing exploit details in a public issue.
